How to Play

WASD move · Shift sprint · C crouch · Space jump or swim · Left mouse mine or attack · Right mouse place, eat, open chests and doors, use table or furnace · Arrow keys also look · E inventory and crafting · V third person · F fly (creative) · 1-9 or wheel for hotbar · Esc pause. Creative: double-tap Space to fly (Space up, C or Shift down).
